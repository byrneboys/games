Killer Rules

1. Target Number: Each player is assigned or picks a target number or dart board sector.

2. Becoming a Killer: Hit your target to become a "Killer". Once active, you can target opponent lives!

3. Turn Controls: When it's your turn, press HIT (Safe) if you made a successful shot to keep your lives intact and pass turn, or MISS (-1 Life) to lose 1 heart.

4. Winning: The last surviving player with remaining lives wins the match!
